sudo is granted via the group. Passwordless sudo writes a visudo-validated, app-managed /etc/sudoers.d/90-explorer-<user> drop-in (NOPASSWD:ALL) — a significant reduction in security. /etc/sudoers and your own /etc/sudoers.d files are never edited. A NOPASSWD* badge means passwordless sudo is configured outside Explorer (e.g. an existing /etc/sudoers.d file); manage those where they live.
