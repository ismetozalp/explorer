# Explorer v4.4.1

- **Resizable AI-view columns.** Grab the divider on the inner edge of the repo
  **tree** or the **code-census** panel and drag to change its width. Your sizes
  are remembered and come back on reload; the diff pane in the middle flexes to
  fill whatever's left.
- **Fixed the census table cutting off its last column.** The language table
  could clip the **Cx** column at the pane edge. It now scrolls sideways when it
  doesn't fit — and widening the pane shows it directly.
